History

Whitewater Rafting was founded in 2012 in the heart of Brotas, a region famous for its breathtaking rivers and lush natural landscapes. What began as a small family business offering weekend rafting trips quickly grew into one of the most recognized adventure tourism companies in the state of São Paulo. The founders, inspired by their passion for outdoor sports and environmental conservation, built the company around safety, sustainability, and unforgettable experiences.

Over the years, Whitewater Rafting as expanded its operations, adding new routes along the Jacaré-Pepira River, technical training programs for guides, and partnerships with local eco-lodges. Today, the company welcomes visitors from all over Brazil and abroad, providing thrilling rafting excursions while promoting respect for nature and local culture. With a team of highly trained instructors and a strong commitment to responsible tourism, Whitewater Rafting continues to shape Brotas as the adventure capital of São Paulo.
